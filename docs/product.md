# Product Overview

## What is OpenSen?

**OpenSen** (Open Sentence) is a language learning ecosystem focused on English fluency through **sentence and phrase chunking**.

Instead of building sentences from isolated vocabulary and grammar rules at speaking time, learners internalize ready-made language units — whole sentences and native pre-assembled phrases — so retrieval feels automatic.

## Learning approach

### Chunking method

Learners memorize **chunks**: fixed or semi-fixed expressions that native speakers use without conscious assembly.

Examples of chunk types:

- Full sentences: *"Could I get a table for two?"*
- Collocations and frames: *"I'm looking forward to …"*, *"It depends on …"*
- Situation-specific routines: greetings, ordering, travel, small talk

### Why chunks

| Traditional path | Chunking path |
|------------------|---------------|
| Word → grammar → assemble at runtime | Pattern → retrieve at runtime |
| High cognitive load while speaking | Lower load; focus on meaning and flow |
| Often sounds unnatural | Closer to native rhythm and phrasing |

## Target outcome

A learner who has built a personal chunk library can:

- Respond in common situations without translating word-by-word
- Swap slot patterns (*"I'd like a …"*, *"Do you have any …?"*) with new vocabulary
- Maintain practice through spaced repetition and portable exports (e.g. Anki)
